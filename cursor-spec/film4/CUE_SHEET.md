# Cursor V4: what changed from V3

These changes respond to the client's notes on V3: "the music is really out of place, make it fit", the intro, and too many sound effects for how little is happening on screen.

## Music fit

| | V3 | V4 |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | The song's two hardest hits (0.139, 0.578) landed on two small keycaps | The hits **are the titles**: black, then **IDEAS.** on hit 1 and **INTO REALITY.** on hit 2 |
| Intro groove | Visual beats drifted against the 808 | ⌘K on the 808 at 1.40, code flies in at 2.60, locks on the 808 at 3.00, and the camera rush builds with the hi-hats from 4.20 into the ember at 5.40 (the sub drops out) |
| Ending | The song was muted for a beat, restarted mid-loop, then faded: the "out of place" feel | At the bar line at 36.18 the song **jumps to its own final bar** (12 ms crossfade). Its real 2-beat break plays under the ember, and its **last 808 hit (36.96) is the lockup** (36.97). Then the song ends naturally, at 37.84 |
| Ducking | Up to 4.5 dB under every SFX (pumping) | At most 2.5 dB, so the song keeps its groove |
| Length | 40.0 s | 39.3 s (no dead air after the song's end) |

Drops 1, 2 and 3 (6.90, 19.56, 32.22) are unchanged: the ember detonates into the logo, the prompt is sent, and TO CREATION.

# Cursor V3: music-locked beat sheet

40.0 s · 1920×1080 · 60 fps.

**Music:** LONOWN "worry", supplied by the client. It runs at 151.67 BPM (beat 0.3956 s, bar 1.5824 s). Every cue in `cues.js` sits on the song's beats, breaks or drops. `audio.py` expects the track at `music/LONOWN_worry.wav`; it is not committed (the track isn't ours to redistribute).

**SFX:** taken from the client's library in `sfx_library/`:
- the SATISYING bloops and ding, plus synth bloops matched to them;
- the Premium pack (basscrack, cine strike, alienated warps, distorting glitch, ripple textures);
- the SFX pack whooshes and impacts, and the UI SaaS pops.

Everything is low-passed so nothing is hissy.

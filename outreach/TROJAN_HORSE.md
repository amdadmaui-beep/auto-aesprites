# The "Trojan Horse" outreach strategy (saved Nov 13)
Notes for AVE Studio: we're in Cagayan de Oro (not Davao). Jeff avoids video calls, so close by email or voice note. Our proof clients: Jackie Kashian, Debi Bradshaw, Brenda Pontiff. Price floor $25. Ask their budget first.

## 1. Pick 15 dream targets
5 comedians, 5 podcasters, 5 YouTubers you genuinely enjoy watching.

## 2. Prove value before asking for money
Creators get dozens of "Hey bro I can edit your videos" DMs a day and delete them all.
- Take a 45-second clip from their latest YouTube video or podcast episode (for a private sample only; never post it yourself).
- Edit it to perfection:
  - Comedian: clean the room audio, punchy animated captions, zoom on the punchlines.
  - Creator: tighten pacing, cut dead air, sharp sound design and B-roll.
- Send the finished vertical clip as an unlisted Google Drive or Frame.io link.

## 3. Scripts
**A. Comedians (IG DM):**
> Yo [Name], that bit about [joke topic] on your page killed me. I noticed you haven't chopped it into a vertical clip yet, so I cleaned up the audio, paced it out and added punchy captions for you [Link]. Feel free to post it, no strings attached. I run an editing studio for comics and have two spots open next month if you want me to handle your weekly clips.

**B. YouTubers and podcasters (email or X DM):**
> Hey [Name], huge fan of your episode with [Guest/Topic]. The story at the 14-minute mark is viral gold, so I pulled it and edited a high-retention short for your Shorts/Reels [Link]. No charge, just wanted to show you what I can do. If you dig the style, happy to take editing off your plate.
(Original says "hop on a 5-minute call". We offer email or a voice note instead.)

## 4. The 4 angles that make creators buy (money, tickets, views, time)
- **Ticket-selling (comedians):** "I turn your 1-hour club sets into 15 viral crowd-work clips a month, built to push followers to your tour link so you sell out your next weekend run."
- **Zero-effort system (podcasters):** "Drop your raw Drive link every Tuesday. By Thursday you have 1 full episode + 10 vertical clips ready to post. You don't even have to talk to me unless you want revisions."
- **Second channel / found money (mid-size YouTubers with no Shorts):** "You're leaving views and AdSense on the table by ignoring Shorts. Give me your back catalog and I'll run your short-form channel hands-off."
- **Agency subcontractor (backdoor):** pitch agencies and overloaded lead editors as a reliable white-label editor. One agency can feed 20 videos a month.

## 5. Upgrade: send a 3-point mini-audit with the clip
1. **Problem:** "Your last 3 episodes had great stories, but your Reels open with slow 'ums' and intro banter, so people scroll."
2. **Fix:** "I took the clip from 18:20, cut straight to the punchline in the first 2 seconds, and paced the captions to your delivery."
3. **Proof:** "Here's the finished file [Link]. Post it tonight and watch the first-3-second retention."

## 6. Be proactive with clients
Every Monday, check each client's channel, feed or Patreon and pitch: "Minutes 12:10, 24:45 and 41:00 are gold for Reels. Want me to chop those three up this week?"
(The "9 out of 10 say yes" claim is optimistic; do it anyway, it creates work.)

## 7. The website is a CLOSING tool, not an opener
- Email #1: no links (spam filters, decision fatigue). Mention clients plus a low-friction question.
- Email #2 (after "sure"): the sample link + website (pricing/packages).
- Above the fold: 3 instant-play vertical clips from clients with results, not a "Welcome" banner. (Saved for later.)

## 8. Setup
- Separate sending email on a cheap lookalike domain (e.g. get[studio].com). Max 25–30 targeted emails a day. (Later, once replies come.)
- Hit list of 150 warm targets, each passing all 3:
  1. posts weekly (YouTube, IG or TikTok)
  2. visibly makes money (tour dates, Patreon, sponsors, 20k+ views)
  3. weak hooks or captions on their short-form, or no vertical clips at all

## 9. Daily routine (Days 4–30): about 2 hours a day, never put links in email #1
**Step 1: no-link opener (15 new prospects a day)**
> Subject: quick clip idea for [Name]
> Hey [Name], I edit vertical clips for [Client 1] and [Client 2]. I watched your latest [episode/set] on [topic], and the 40-second moment at [timestamp] would crush on Reels and Shorts. Mind if I cut a quick sample for you to check out? No strings attached.
**Step 2: when they say "sure":** cut that exact clip and reply with the Drive link + website.
**Step 3: follow-ups, 3 more touches over 10 days**
- Day 3: "Just bumping this in case you're on the road. Still happy to cut that [topic] clip."
- Day 7: "Quick question: are you already working with a short-form team, or still handling clips yourself?"
- Day 12: "Last ping from me! If you ever get backed up turning your sets/episodes into weekly Reels, keep my info handy."

## 10. Close the monthly retainer (by email or voice note)
- Don't ask for 3 months up front. Offer a paid trial: "Let's do a 2-week paid sprint: 8 clips for [half-month price]. If you love the views and the workflow, we roll into the standard 16-clip monthly retainer on the 1st."
- Sell "done-for-you clipping": "Drop your raw episode or set in Drive. We watch it all, pull the best moments, edit them with captions and sound, and hand you a ready-to-post folder every week."
  Bad pitch: "I edit Reels for $25 a clip." It sounds like more work for them.

## 12. Who actually pays (the "Goldilocks zone")
- YouTube/podcasts: 15k–150k subscribers (they earn money but can't afford a $4k/month editor).
- Comedians: 10k–100k IG/TikTok followers WITH active tour dates.
- Skip 1k (no money) and 1M+ (they have in-house teams).

## 13. Best lead sources
- **YouTube filter:** "podcast" + niche → Upload date: this week, Duration: over 20 min → channels with 15k–150k subs whose Shorts are bad or stale.
- **Comedy club lineups:** Comedy Store, Laugh Factory, Zanies, Helium, Improv weekend calendars → every headliner and feature is touring.
- **Spotify/Apple charts:** comedy, business and self-improvement shows ranked #50–200; the email is usually in the description.
- **X/Threads live search:** "need an editor", "looking for a clip editor", "hate editing", "behind on editing" → sort by Latest, reply within 10 minutes.

## 14. 10-second disqualification (skip if any fails)
- Active in the last 14 days?
- Can pay $480/month (sponsors, Patreon, merch, coaching, tours)?
- Actually needs us (no strong editor already)? Look for great content in boring packaging.

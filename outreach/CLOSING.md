# Closing playbook: turning leads into paying clients

Goal: 1 new paying client (besides Jackie) by **Nov 15, 2026**.

## Why deals slipped
1. Leads had no budget (small comics who love the sample but can't pay).
2. Price was given before asking their budget, and the first offer was too big ($120 trial).
3. Too slow: Aiden hired in-house before the call happened.

## Rule 1: Go after people who already spend money
Prioritize leads with at least one of these **budget signals**:
- They posted a job ad or "hiring an editor" (they've already decided to pay)
- They have a manager, agent, or publicist
- They tour, sell tickets, or have a Patreon or paid membership
- Their podcast has sponsors or ads
- They're a business: agency, production company, real estate agent, coach selling a course
- They already pay someone (an "edited by" credit, or polished clips that suddenly stopped)

No budget signal? Still send the sample, but don't spend hours on them.

## Rule 2: Ask before you price
When they like the sample, **don't quote yet.** Ask:
> So glad you like it! Quick question so I can suggest the right setup: roughly how many clips a month are you thinking, and do you have a budget in mind?

- They give a number → fit an option to it (see the ladder below).
- They say "what do you charge?" → give the **starter** first, then the ladder.

## Price script (user's rule, Nov 11): ask budget first, anchor at $25
When they ask the price:
> Happy to work with what fits you. Do you have a budget in mind per clip or per month? For reference, a clip like the sample I sent (or the ones on our site) is $25.

- They give a number at or above $25 a clip → say yes and start.
- They want ongoing work → offer monthly: "8 clips a month for $180" (saves them $20).
- They're below $25 → stay friendly: "No worries, the sample's yours to keep. If the budget opens up, I'm here." Never go under $25 a clip.

## Rule 3: The price ladder (always start small)

| Option | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | 1 more clip, same style | **$30** |
| **Weekly** | 4 clips/month | **$120/month** |
| **Growth** | 8 clips/month | **$220/month** (save $20) |
| **Daily-ish** | 12+ clips/month | **$300/month** (save $60) |

Script:
> It's $30 a clip, clipping and editing included. Most people start with one more clip to see it working on their page. If you want to keep it going, 4 a month is $120, or 8 a month is $220.

**The first paid order should be small ($30).** A $30 yes is easy. A $120 yes is a decision they'll "think about". Once they've paid once, upgrading is easy.

If they say "too expensive":
> Totally get it. What would work for you? I can do 2 clips for $50 to start, and you can add more later.
Never go below **$25 a clip**. If they can't do $25, say "no worries, the sample's yours" and stay friendly. They might come back.

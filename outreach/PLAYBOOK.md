# AVE Studio client-hunting playbook

Goal: first paying client by **November 15, 2026**.
Offer: free sample edit first, then **$30 per video (clipping + editing included)**.
Price rule: **never say the price first. Only when they ask.**
Call rule: **avoid calls; keep it in DMs/email.** The sample does the selling. If a serious buyer insists and it's unavoidable, agree to a short call (see section 6).

---

## 1. Who we go after (in this order)

| Priority | Who | Why they convert |
|---|---|---|
| 1 HOT | People who already posted "looking for a clip editor" | They already decided to pay. We just need to be faster and easier. |
| 2 WARM | Comedians who self-released a special on YouTube in the last ~60 days, with 1k–50k followers | 40–70 min of fresh footage, almost nobody clipping it. Huge, obvious gap. |
| 3 WARM | Small video podcasts (500–20k subs) with full episodes but few/no Shorts | Every episode is wasted clip material. |
| 4 COLD | Everyone else | Only when 1–3 are exhausted. |

**Skip:** big names with teams, people posting slick daily clips already, anyone over ~150k followers.

## 2. The move that wins without calls: "sample first"

Don't ask "want a free sample?". **Make the sample first, then send it.**
A finished clip of *their own joke* in the first message is impossible to ignore, and it
means there's nothing to "hop on a call" about. They watch, they like it, they ask the price.

How:
1. Watch their YouTube special, pick the strongest 30–60 s bit (a story with a clear hook + punchline).
2. Cut it in our style: hook in first 2 s, captions on the beat, punchline not spoiled by captions, 9:16.
3. Upload as an unlisted link (Google Drive / YouTube unlisted / Streamable). Watermark "AVE Studio" small in a corner.
4. Send the DM with the link.

Do this for the **top 5–10 prospects** (fits 4–5). For the rest, send the "offer a sample" version.

## 3. Volume math for Nov 15

Typical cold DM reply rate for a *customized* message with a free sample: roughly 5–15%.
Of people who reply and see a sample, a meaningful share buy at $30 (it's cheap).

- **Send 15–25 customized messages per day** (DM + email where available).
- **Make 2–3 pre-made samples per day** for the best fits.
- Over 14 days: ~250 messages, ~30–40 samples. That's what gets one paying client with high odds.

Honest note: no one can promise a 99% hit rate. What makes it near-certain is
**volume × personalization × sample-first**. Skip any of the three and it slows down a lot.

## 5. Message templates (customize the [brackets] every time)

> I run a small clip studio for comedians and I cut that bit into a vertical clip for you, captions and all:
> [link]
> Yours to post free, no strings. If you like it I can do more from the special.

### B. Offer a sample (no pre-made clip)

> Portfolio: https://ave-studio-website.vercel.app/

### D. Podcast

## 6. Replies — copy/paste answers (keep it in text, no calls)

**"How much?"**
> It's $30 per video, and that includes the clipping and the editing. No contract, you only pay for what you order.

**"Can we hop on a call?" / "Let's do a Zoom"**
> Totally get it! I actually keep everything in messages so you always have it in writing and you never have to schedule around me.
> Easiest way to see if we're a fit: send me one set or episode link and I'll turn around a sample in [24–48h]. If you like it, we go from there.
(If they insist once more: "Happy to answer anything here. Fire away, I usually reply within the hour.")
(If it's truly unavoidable for a serious buyer: say yes to a **15-minute** call. Prep: have the sample open, know the price ($30/video incl. clipping + editing), and ask 3 questions: what they post, how often, which clips they like. Then follow up in writing the same day.)

**"What do you need from me?"**
> Just a link to the full set/episode (YouTube, Drive, or Dropbox). Timestamps are a bonus but not needed, we find the best moments ourselves.

**"How fast?"**
> First sample in 24–48 hours. After that, a normal turnaround is [X days] per batch.

**"Can it match my style?"**
> Yes, tell me a creator whose clips you like (or send one) and we'll match the captions and pacing.

**"Do you do revisions?"**
> Yes, one round of changes is included.

**They liked the sample → close**
> Glad you liked it! Want me to do more from [special]? I'd suggest [3–5] clips to start, so you can post a few times this week. Just say the word and I'll get going.

**"Is this AI?"**
> No, every clip is cut by a human editor. We pick the moments, time the captions to your delivery, and keep your timing intact.

## 7. Follow-up cadence

- Day 0: first message.
- Day 3: "Hey [Name], just bumping this in case it got buried. The clip's still yours if you want it: [link]"
- Day 7: last nudge with something new (a second short clip, or a quick note on a fresh post of theirs).
- Then stop. Mark as "no reply" in the tracker.

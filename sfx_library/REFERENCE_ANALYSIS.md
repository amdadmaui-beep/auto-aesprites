## 1 · Cursor Concept Promo: how it's built

**Look**
- Deep navy/indigo dark UI with a faint purple vignette.
- A real-looking Cursor window, with the explorer, editor, and Chat/Agent panel all visible.
- Captions are white, centred, near the bottom. They reveal **word by word**, with the key word in bold or bright ("think", "entire codebase", "One").
- Big statement text sits on dark: "Meet Cursor.", then "Write less. / Build more. / Ship faster." stacking line by line, while the previous line dims to grey.

**Camera and edit**
- Almost no hard cuts: only 2 detected (37.5 s and 48.5 s).
- It moves with slow, continuous push-ins and pans across one big UI, changing focus by zooming into a region (the selection, the inline prompt, the diff, the terminal).
- Transitions are blur and fade dissolves, plus one hard cut from dark UI to the white app at 37.5 s.

**Story beats (time → screen)**

| t | Screen | Sound in the mix |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2.7 | "What if your code editor could think with you?" types in over the blurred UI | A sub drop and a soft ping on the opening word, then an airy whoosh at 1.2 |
| 2.7–3.4 | Hand-off into the UI ("Meet") | A noise riser and broadband hit at 2.75–3.3 |
| 3.4–6.5 | "Meet Cursor." / "AI built directly into your editor." | Soft pops on each line (4.06, 4.44, 5.12, 5.61) |
| 6.5–7.9 | Push into the editor | A wide whoosh at 6.67 |
| 7.9–9 | Code block selected | A click at 7.97, then a pop and click at 8.6–8.9 |
| 9–12.4 | Inline prompt, "Describe what you want." typed | A pop at 9.22, a blip at 9.57, and a **key-rain typing burst** from 9.7–11.0 |

**Takeaways for us**
1. Sound only marks **meaningful UI events**: a selection, Enter, a diff landing, a checkmark, a file updated. The typing is a dense, soft "key-rain", not loud single keys.
2. **Tonal pings and blips** (about 1–3 kHz, short and pure) are the signature "AI did something" sound.
3. Whooshes are **airy and wide**. They happen only on camera moves and transitions, never on text.
4. The music carries the energy. The SFX sit on top at a moderate level and are crisp rather than heavy.

## 2 · Perplexity ad (Trove Studio): what to borrow

- **A morph-driven open:** a dot becomes the text caret, the caret blinks, and the logo blooms out of it and morphs into the wordmark. Everything grows out of one element.
- **UI built in front of you:** the search box slides in, the cursor clicks a dropdown, a query is typed with a yellow highlight, Send is clicked, then a loading state, then a result with an orange glow.
- **Every interaction gets a crisp click.** Clicks are tight and land on the music's 16th grid (2.66, 2.91, 3.16, 3.91, 4.04, 4.16, 4.41 → spaced 0.125–0.25 s). Swells sit under the morphs (5.2, 16.9), and a single whoosh carries the loading transition (13.06).
- **The ending:** the caret becomes the lockup, a flame, then the studio logo, with one clean ping at 21.2.

## 3 · Background music: LONOWN – "worry"

`music_beats_LONOWN_worry.json` holds the full map.

**Tempo:** **151.7 BPM** (beat 0.3956 s), felt in half time (about 76 BPM). A bar is **1.582 s**. Bar accents fall on the grid **1.376 + n × 1.582 s**: 2.95, 4.54, 6.12, 7.70, 9.28, …

**Sections:**

| t | Section | Energy |
|---|---|---|
| 0.00–0.6 | Two hard opening hits (0.14, 0.58) | **The strongest accents in the song** |
| 0.6–4.94 | Intro: bass and sub, no highs | Mid |
| 4.94–6.5 | **Build: the sub drops out** | Tension |
| 6.5–19.15 | Loop A: full beat (8 bars) | High |
| 19.15–31.81 | Loop B (8 bars) | High |
| 31.81–44.47 | Loop C (8 bars) | High |
| 44.47–57.13 | Loop D | High |
| 57.13–71 | Loop E, then the end | High |

**Patterns:** each 8-bar loop ends with a one-bar **fill and sub dip** (around 17.6, 30.2, 42.9, 55.5), which is a natural place for a transition or a hit. The loops are almost identical, so variety has to come from the picture and the SFX.

**Edit plan for 40 s:** the opening hits sync to the first title smash. The 4.94–6.5 build becomes the suction into the logo reveal, and the drop at about 6.5 is the logo or "MEET CURSOR." Section changes at 19.15 and 31.81 mark Agent and the hero dashboard. The fill around 30.2 sets up the montage, and a hard music stop on a bar line around 38.1 lands the lockup.

## 4 · SATISYING.mp4: the target sound

This file is byte-identical to the UI SaaS pack video. Its first 3 seconds are:
- **About 22 liquid "bloops" in 2 seconds.** Each falls fast in pitch, from about 0.7–2.5 kHz down to about 100 Hz, over a warm low body. They come every 40–130 ms and swell toward the middle.
- **One bright "ding"** to confirm, at 2.7 s.

This is the "creamy and satisfying" reference. My V2 plops glided *up* in pitch, the wrong direction, and were too sparse. V3 will use the real bloops plus a matched synth (`satisfying/synth_bloop_burst_match.wav`) for:
- bursts when UI assembles (cards, code lines, chart bars, letters),
- single bloops on taps and snaps,
- the ding on confirmations (Tab accept, ✓, Ready).

## 6 · Music-app promo (r5): sound-design notes

- It uses the same bloop family: downward pitch drops, here on taps and hearts.
- Soft noise "clouds" swell under the UI morphs.
- The bass-heavy music cuts out completely between sections (3.2–6.6 s), so the SFX play solo. That's a strong contrast trick we can use before the drop.
